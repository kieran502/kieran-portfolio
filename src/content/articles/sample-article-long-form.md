This is a sample article. It exists so the article template can be reviewed with realistic structure, and it does not represent Kieran's views. Delete it, or set `draft: true`, once the first real article is ready.

The opening paragraph of an article sits directly under the title and summary. Paragraphs are set at a comfortable reading size with a line length of around seventy characters, which holds up well for long pieces on both a phone and a desktop screen.

## Section headings

Second-level headings divide an article into its main sections. They use the same serif as the page title, so a reader skimming the page can find their place quickly.

### Sub-headings

Third-level headings break a long section into smaller parts. Headings should always step down one level at a time, which keeps the page usable with a screen reader.

## Lists

Bulleted lists work for items in no particular order:

- A short point
- A slightly longer point that runs on far enough to wrap onto a second line on narrow screens
- A final point

Numbered lists work for steps:

1. Write the article in Word, ChatGPT or anywhere else.
2. Paste the text into the article file.
3. Publish.

## Quotations and emphasis

Use **bold** sparingly for key terms and *italics* for titles or light emphasis. Links look like [this link to the work page](/work/).

> A pull quote or quotation is set apart like this. Only quote real people, with their permission.
